# Analysed papers (268)

The corpus: papers published at IEEE S&P, USENIX Security and NDSS, 2023–2026, from the venues'
open-access proceedings. **No PDF or full text is in this repository.** The corpus contains no ACM CCS
papers; CCS guidance in the skill is convention, not measurement.

Rules were fitted on the 241 papers marked `fit`. The 27 marked **held-out** were removed from every
rule and exemplar and are used only in `skills/security-conf-writing/evals/`.

Paper types follow `skills/security-conf-writing/references/routing.md`:
A1 attack · A2 defense · A3 measurement · A4 system · A5 analysis · A6 SoK · A7 dataset.

Machine-readable version: [`data/papers.csv`](data/papers.csv).
